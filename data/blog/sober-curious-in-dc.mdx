For a long time, the default setting for making friends in your 20s and 30s was "let's grab a drink." But a massive shift is happening across Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia. 

The "sober-curious" movement is exploding. Whether you've cut out alcohol entirely, or you just don't want to spend $18 on a cocktail every time you leave your apartment, you need social options that don't revolve around a bar tab.

Here are 5 ways to make friends and socialize in the DMV without the hangover.

## 1. The Cafe Crawl
Instead of a bar crawl, organize a cafe crawl. D.C. has an incredible specialty coffee scene. Spend a Saturday morning walking between spots like Compass Coffee, Tryst, or The Wydown. It’s daytime, the lighting is good, and you can actually hear the people you are talking to.

## 2. Zero-Proof Bars
The mocktail scene is finally getting the respect it deserves. Places like Binge Bar on H Street are entirely alcohol-free spaces designed specifically for socializing, live music, and great vibes without the booze.

## 3. Co-Op Board Game Nights
Board game cafes like Board Room (which allows outside food and has great non-alcoholic options) or Labyrinth Games host open-play nights. The game acts as the perfect social lubricant, entirely removing the pressure to constantly keep a conversation going.

## 4. Active Volunteering
Few things bond people faster than working together for a good cause. Organizations like DC Central Kitchen or local park cleanups through the Rock Creek Conservancy attract kind, community-minded people. 

## 5. Join a "Chill & Coffee" Community
If you are tired of trying to organize these outings yourself, tap into a community that already prioritizes them.
